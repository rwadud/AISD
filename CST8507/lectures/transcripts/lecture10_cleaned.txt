Retriever-reader architecture deals with this specific type of question-answer system, where we just give the model the question and let the model extract the answer for us.

In retriever-reader architecture, we have two main components. The first is the retriever. We feed the retriever with our data source, unstructured data, a huge amount of data. We want to create a question-answer system, for example, for grade 12 biology. I have many books related to this topic, so I feed the retriever with all these unstructured documents. When we give the question, the retriever tries to find a similarity between the question and the data it has, and the output of the retriever is the top related documents to my question, top 10, top 5, whatever we decide.

This is the role of the retriever. It receives a question, and it stores a huge amount of data I want to work on for a specific domain. The retriever returns the most relevant documents related to my question. It will not answer my question.

Then it feeds the reader with these documents. The reader is a normal reading comprehension. The reader has the question and the context from the retriever. This part is normal reading comprehension.
